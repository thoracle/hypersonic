THE SONG IS THE LEVEL

There is no authored content. The music you pick raises the terrain, sends the enemies, sets the palette, decides where the boss goes and how long the level lasts. Same track, same world. Every time.
You ride a rail. You do not steer the ship — you steer the reticle. The game hears every hit a few seconds before you do, so your shots land on the music, not near it.

CONTROLS

MOUSEAim. The ship flies itself.
HOLD CLICKSweep-lock every target you pass over. Up to eight.
RELEASEFire the volley. On the beat to build the chain.
SPACEWhile holding: stack an extra lock.
EOverdrive. Clears the field. Limited.
VView: retro → wire → shaded.
ESCPause. Again to fly on.
On a phone: stick aims, FIRE holds the sweep, OD spends an overdrive, and II at the bottom is pause — there is no ESC key. Landscape only.

COLOUR IS INTENT

REDAttacks you. Hunters, swarmers, tanks, shells, boss hulls and bays.
GOLDNever does. Pods, mine-layers, mines. A mine is a hazard you fly into.
BLUEYours. Your ship, the mothership, data nodes, the ring that evolves you.
MAGENTAPower. The overdrive ring and its pips. Nothing hostile is ever this colour.
Anything too far to shoot is dimmed to a ghost. When it lights up, it is lockable. The box on a target reports your relationship to it: faint white in range, solid when locked, pulsing when a missile is already running.

SCORING

+2000Accuracy. Squared — sloppy volleys are expensive.
+1000Items. Rings collected. Also squared.
+1000Analyzation. Data nodes opened.
Rhythm chain: release within a sixteenth of a beat and the chain climbs. At three and six links your lock capacity grows from eight to ten to twelve. One off-beat release resets it to nothing. This is the difference between shooting at the music and playing it.

FLYING WELL

Release on the beat, always. Shoot the shells — they are targets and they are points. Pop pods at a distance. Do not ignore the blue: a run that shoots perfectly and collects nothing leaves a third of its points on the table.
